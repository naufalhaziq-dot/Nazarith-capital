THE NAZARITH METHOD / OPERATING FRAMEWORK

Process before prediction.

A durable trading process is built through repeatable decisions, measured risk, and rigorous post-trade auditing. The objective is never to predict every market turn, but to respond consistently when structural confluence presents an authentic edge.

01 / PREPARE

Define the Context

Map higher-timeframe order flow, liquidity pools, key invalidation levels, and impending macro economic events before considering an order.

02 / EXECUTE

Control the Risk

Calculate lot size strictly to risk percent (0.5%–1%). Establish the invalidation price in advance; every trade must have a defined price where the thesis is wrong.

03 / REVIEW

Learn from Evidence

Record pre-trade and post-trade chart screenshots, emotional state, and mistake tags. Audit a 100-trade sample to separate statistical edge from short-term variance.
