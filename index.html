THE NAZARITH METHOD / OPERATING FRAMEWORK

Process before
prediction.

A durable trading process is built through repeatable decisions, measured risk, and honest review. The objective is not to predict every market move, but to respond consistently when conditions meet a defined plan.

01 / PREPARE

Define the context

Map higher-timeframe structure, liquidity, key levels, and the events that may change market conditions before considering an entry.

02 / EXECUTE

Control the risk

Set invalidation, position size, and acceptable exposure in advance. Every position must have a clear reason to exist and a defined point where the thesis is wrong.

03 / REVIEW

Learn from evidence

Record the setup, execution, and outcome. Review a meaningful sample of trades to distinguish a repeatable edge from short-term noise.

05 / ACCESS

Build what lasts.

The beginning of a disciplined process is a decision to think beyond the present. NAZARITH CAPITAL is built around that decision.
